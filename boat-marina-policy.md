## 5. Before Leaving the Dock

Go through this checklist before every trip:

- [ ] Check the weather and water conditions. **Do not go out** in storm, small-craft, or high-wind warnings.
- [ ] Tell someone on shore where you're going and when you'll be back (a "float plan").
- [ ] Count life jackets: there must be **one that fits each person** on board.
- [ ] Check that the fire extinguisher, flares, horn/whistle, and first-aid kit are on board.
- [ ] Check fuel, oil, and battery levels.
- [ ] Check the bilge (bottom of the boat) for water and make sure the drain plug is in.
- [ ] Test the navigation lights if you might be out near dark.
- [ ] For gas engines: run the blower fan for **at least 4 minutes** before starting the engine.
- [ ] Unplug the shore power cord and stow it before untying.
- [ ] Make sure all passengers are seated and lines are clear of the propeller before pulling out.

## 6. Safety Rules

1. **No alcohol or drugs** for the person driving the boat. Boating under the influence is illegal.
2. Never carry more people or weight than the boat's capacity plate allows.
3. Children must wear a life jacket at all times while on board or on the dock. (Local law may set a specific age.)
4. All passengers should wear life jackets when the boat is moving fast, in rough water, or at night.
5. Turn the engine **off** when anyone is swimming near the back of the boat.
6. Use the engine kill switch lanyard while driving.
7. Keep a proper lookout and follow navigation rules (stay to the right in channels, give way as required).
8. Obey all speed limits and **no-wake zones**.
9. Return to the marina before dark unless the owner has approved night use.

---

## 7. Guest Rules

1. The operator is responsible for every guest's safety and behavior.
2. Guests must follow the operator's instructions at all times.
3. No running or diving from the dock or the boat while at the marina.
4. Pets must be on a leash on the docks and cleaned up after.
5. Guests may not bring glass bottles on board.
6. Guests must stay off other people's boats and docks.

---

## 8. Marina Rules

Follow all posted marina rules. These are the most common ones:

1. **No-wake speed** inside the marina.
2. Respect quiet hours: ________ to ________.
3. Keep the dock and walkways clear: no hoses, cords, coolers, or gear left in the way.
4. **Never dump sewage** (toilet waste) in the marina or nearby water. Use the pump-out station.
5. Put trash, oil, and old batteries in the marina's proper bins.
6. Do not fuel from portable cans at the slip unless the marina allows it.
7. Do major repairs only where and when the marina permits.
8. Park only in the areas the marina allows for slip holders and guests.
9. Do not live aboard or stay overnight unless the marina permits it.

---

## 9. Fueling

1. Fuel only at the marina's fuel dock or another approved fuel dock.
2. Before fueling: turn off the engine, all electronics, and stoves. Have passengers get off the boat.
3. **No smoking** or open flames anywhere near the fuel dock.
4. Close hatches and doors while fueling so fumes don't build up inside.
5. Wipe up any spill right away and tell the fuel dock staff.
6. After fueling (gas engines): open hatches, sniff for fumes, and run the blower for **at least 4 minutes** before starting.

## 10. Returning to the Slip

- [ ] Tie the boat securely with dock lines at the bow, stern, and spring lines.
- [ ] Put out fenders so the boat doesn't rub the dock or the boat next to it.
- [ ] Plug in the shore power (if used) and make sure the cord isn't in the water.
- [ ] Turn off the engine, lights, and electronics. Turn the battery switch to ________.
- [ ] Remove the key and return it to: ________.
- [ ] Take all trash and personal items off the boat.
- [ ] Rinse off salt water and wipe down seats if needed.
- [ ] Put the cover on and close all hatches, windows, and doors.
- [ ] Write the trip in the boat log (date, operator, engine hours, fuel added, any problems).
- [ ] Tell the owner about **any** problems or damage, even small ones.

## 11. Care & Maintenance

1. Only the owner (or someone the owner approves) may arrange repairs.
2. Report anything broken, leaking, or not working right away.
3. Do not make any changes to the boat (equipment, wiring, decals, etc.) without the owner's approval.
4. The owner keeps a maintenance schedule for engine service, bottom cleaning, and safety gear checks.

---

## 12. Accidents & Damage

1. **If anyone is hurt or in danger:** call **911** or use **VHF Channel 16** right away.
2. Help any other boat or person in trouble if you can do so safely.
3. Tell the owner as soon as possible about any accident, damage, or near-miss.
4. Take photos and write down what happened, the time, the place, and the names and phone numbers of anyone involved.
5. Serious boating accidents (such as an injury, a death, a missing person, or major damage) usually must be **reported to the local boating authority**. In the U.S., this is your state's boating agency. Check your local rules.
6. Do not admit fault or make deals with others involved. Let the owner and insurance company handle it.
7. Unless agreed otherwise in writing, the operator may be responsible for damage caused by careless use or breaking this policy.

## 14. Storms & Hurricanes

1. Follow the marina's storm or hurricane plan.
2. When a big storm is coming, the owner (or someone they choose) will:
   - Add extra and longer dock lines and extra fenders.
   - Remove canvas, cushions, and anything loose that could blow away.
   - Close all hatches and seacocks that should be closed.
   - Make sure the bilge pump and batteries are working.
   - Take photos of the boat before the storm for insurance.
3. Nobody should stay on the boat during a storm.

---

## 15. Not Allowed

- Running the boat while drunk or high
- Letting someone not on the approved operator list drive
- Carrying more people than the capacity plate allows
- Racing, towing people, or tubing unless the owner approves
- Fishing or swimming in areas where it's banned
- Illegal activity of any kind on or near the boat
- Using the boat for paid trips or rentals without the owner's written approval and proper licenses/insurance

---

## 16. Breaking the Rules

If someone doesn't follow this policy, the owner may:

1. Give a warning.
2. Remove them from the approved operator list.
3. Ask them to pay for any damage, fines, or marina fees they caused.
