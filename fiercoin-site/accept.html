Accept FIRC

Take FierCoin payments at your shop, stall or website. Free, no bank, no sign-up.

Why accept it

Free

No card fees, no monthly costs. Network fees are a tiny fraction of a FIRC.

Fast

A payment shows up in seconds and is confirmed in about 2.5 minutes.

No sign-up

No account, no bank, no paperwork. Just the free app.

Set it up in 3 minutes

  1. Install the free Android app on the shop phone.
  2. On the Balance tab, do the 2 safety steps: set a password and save a backup.
  3. Tap Receive. Show the QR code to the customer, or print it and stick it by the till.
  4. When the customer pays, check the History tab. Wait for 1 confirmation for big amounts.

Tip: set your own price in FIRC, e.g. "Coffee: 20 FIRC" or "10% off when you pay in FIRC". Since FIRC has no official price, you decide what it's worth to you.

Good to know

  • FIRC's value can change or stay at zero. Only accept what you're comfortable with.
  • Payments can't be reversed, so refunds are done by sending FIRC back.
  • In the US, accepting crypto for goods can count as income for taxes. Keep a simple record.
