What really helps

  1. Tell people you know. A friend telling a friend beats any ad.
  2. Show it working. Open the app, tap Mine, and let them see a block get found.
  3. Help one person start. Set up their wallet with them, and send them their first FIRC.
  4. Ask a local shop to accept FIRC, even just for a small discount.
  5. Stay honest. Never promise a price or profit. That’s what scams do, and it isn’t what FierCoin is about.

Please don’t spam

Posting the same message in lots of groups, servers or comment sections gets accounts banned and makes FierCoin look like a scam. One honest post where it belongs is worth more than a hundred copies.
