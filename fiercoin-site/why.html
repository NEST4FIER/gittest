Freedom Is Everyone’s Right

That’s what FIER in FierCoin stands for. Money runs on trust, and that trust belongs to the people.

Money runs on trust

A dollar is a piece of paper, or a number in a bank’s computer. It’s worth something because we all trust it, and behind that, because we trust our nation. That trust doesn’t belong to a government or a bank. It belongs to the people who give it.

FierCoin is built on the same idea, with nobody in the middle. It’s worth whatever free people decide it’s worth, and nobody can take that choice away from them.

Why now

More of our lives go digital every year: our IDs, our payments, our records. That can make life easier. But some plans around the world, like digital IDs tied to every payment, or government digital money that can be programmed, could also make it possible to watch what people buy, or to switch their money off.

We believe technology should serve people, not control them. A free people should never need permission to spend their own money.

What makes FierCoin different

Nobody owns it

The code is open for anyone to read, check or copy. No company or government runs it.

Anyone can mine it

Free, on a phone or a computer. The same rules for everyone, the founder included.

No ID, no bank, no permission

Download the wallet and you’re in. Nobody can turn you down.

Your keys, your coins

Only your backup (your 12 words on iPhone) can move your coins. Nobody can freeze your wallet.

One honest record

Every payment is on one public blockchain that anyone can check. No hidden books.

What FierCoin stands for

Democracy works when people are free: free to speak, free to vote, free to choose. FierCoin is a small way to practice that freedom every day, with money that works because people choose it, not because they’re forced to.

The very first FierCoin block carries a message, written into the blockchain forever:

“FierCoin 13/Nov/2026 Regaining our democracy in the USA”

Freedom is for everyone. Left, right or neither, rich or poor, in any country: FierCoin works the same for everybody. It isn’t part of any party, and it doesn’t collect money for anyone.

Honest limits

  • Not anonymous. Payments are public on the blockchain. People can’t see your name, but once they know an address is yours, they can see its history.
  • Not an investment. FIRC has no price yet and might never have one. Nobody can promise it will be worth money.
  • Not a fundraiser. We don’t sell coins or ask for donations. If someone does in FierCoin’s name, it isn’t us.
  • Not a replacement for democracy. The strongest protection for freedom is still you: vote, speak up, and stay informed.
