About FierCoin

How it started, who holds the coins, and what FierCoin is and isn't. No hype.

What the name means

FIER stands for Freedom Is Everyone’s Right. The coin’s short name, FIRC, is built into every FierCoin address (they start with firc1). Read why FierCoin exists.

How it started

FierCoin launched on 13 November 2026 as a new blockchain made from Litecoin's open-source code. There was no pre-sale, no ICO and no coins created out of thin air. Every FIRC that exists was mined, block by block, by someone running the free wallet.

Genesis message: FierCoin 13/Nov/2026 Regaining our democracy in the USA

Who mined the first coins

Like Bitcoin in 2009, the founder's computer was almost the only miner at first, so the founder holds most of the early coins. We'd rather tell you that than have you find it yourself. Anyone can check every address and balance on the live explorer.

What FierCoin is

  • Its own blockchain, with the same rules style and mining method (Scrypt) as Litecoin.
  • Open source: anyone can read, check or copy the code.
  • Run by the people who run the wallet, not by a company.

What FierCoin isn't

  • Not an investment. FIRC has no price and isn't on any exchange. Nobody is promising it will be worth money.
  • Not for sale by us. We don't sell coins. If anyone offers to sell you FIRC "from the team", it's not us.
  • Not a fundraiser. FierCoin doesn't collect money for any person, party or campaign.

What would give FIRC value

A coin is worth something only when people want it. That comes from:

  1. More miners, so the network is strong and spread out.
  2. People and shops accepting it for real things. Here's how a shop can accept FIRC.
  3. People trading it with each other, which sets its first price.
