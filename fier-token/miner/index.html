    How mining works

    1. Connect MetaMask and switch to the Base network. This page will offer to switch for you.
    2. Press Start mining. Your computer makes millions of guesses, looking for a number that solves the current puzzle.
    3. When it finds one, MetaMask asks you to approve a claim. Approve it and the reward lands in your wallet. Then a new puzzle starts.

    This page never asks for your recovery phrase or private key. Every claim is a normal MetaMask transaction that you approve, and the network fee on Base is usually a fraction of a cent. If someone else solves the puzzle first, your claim is cancelled before it is sent and mining moves on to the next puzzle.
