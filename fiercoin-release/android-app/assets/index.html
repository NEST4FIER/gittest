Help

How do I earn FIRC?
Tap the big Mine button on Home. Each block you find pays 50 FIRC.

Why "waiting"?
Mined coins must wait 100 blocks (about 4 hours) before you can spend them. Every mined coin works this way.

What does "lost" mean?
Another computer found the same block first, so yours didn't count. That's normal.

How does the network work?
Like Bitcoin: your phone connects to other FierCoin computers by itself, and everyone shares one blockchain. That's what lets you send coins to anyone, anywhere. Top right shows how many computers you're connected to.

Updating the app
Back up first, then install the new version and tap Update. Never uninstall first: that deletes the wallet.
