WHAT FIERCOIN IS
----------------
- Its own blockchain, made from Litecoin's code (just like Litecoin).
- Mined with your computer's or phone's processor using "Scrypt", the same mining method as Litecoin.
- 84,000,000 coins total, ever. That's the same as Litecoin.
- Each block pays the miner 50 FIRC. The reward is cut in half every 840,000 blocks (about every 4 years).
- A new block is found about every 2.5 minutes. Difficulty adjusts every hour, so it stays steady when miners come and go.
- Your wallet addresses start with "firc1" (or "F" for older-style ones).
- One network, shared by everyone (like Bitcoin). Every FierCoin app joins it by itself.


GET FIERCOIN (pick your device)
-------------------------------
Everything is free on the FierCoin website:  https://nest4fier.github.io/gittest/fiercoin/

- Windows PC (Windows 10 or 11): download "FierCoin-Setup-Windows.exe".
  A wallet with a built-in miner. Follow Steps 1 to 5 below.
- Android phone (Android 8 or newer): download "FierCoin-Android.apk".
  A wallet with a one-tap miner. See "ON AN ANDROID PHONE" below.
- iPhone: nothing to download. It's a web app that you open in Safari:
  https://68-183-155-121.sslip.io/wallet/    See "ON AN IPHONE" below.

They all use the same network, so you can send coins between any of them.


STEP 1 - INSTALL ON YOUR WINDOWS PC
-----------------------------------
1. Double-click "FierCoin-Setup-Windows.exe".
2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". This happens because the app is new and not "signed".
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Click Next until it finishes, then open "FierCoin Core" from the Start menu.
4. On first launch, keep the default data folder and click OK.
5. If it says "No wallet has been loaded", click "Create a new wallet", type a name
   (for example: main), then click "Create". You only do this once.
6. Allow it through the Windows Firewall if asked, so other people's computers can connect to yours.
7. That's it. FierCoin finds the network by itself and downloads the blockchain (see Step 5).


STEP 2 - MAKE YOUR WALLET SAFE (do this first!)
------------------------------------------------
1. Settings > Encrypt Wallet. Choose a strong password and WRITE IT DOWN on paper.
2. File > Backup Wallet. Save the backup to a USB stick. Keep it somewhere safe.
   (If your PC dies and you have no backup, your coins are gone forever.)
3. Never type your password or share your wallet file on any website or in any chat.


STEP 3 - GET YOUR ADDRESS
-------------------------
1. Click the "Receive" tab.
2. Click "Create new receiving address".
3. Click "Copy Address". It looks like:  firc1q...


STEP 4 - START MINING
---------------------
1. Click Window > Console.
2. Type this (paste YOUR address at the end) and press Enter:

      setgenerate true 2 firc1qYOURADDRESSHERE

   The "2" means it uses 2 processor threads. Use a bigger number to mine faster
   (your PC will get warmer and slower). Try 2 to 4.

3. To check how it's going, type:

      getgenerate

   "hashespersec" is your speed. "blocksfound" counts your wins.

4. To stop mining, type:

      setgenerate false

Note: freshly mined coins are "Immature" until 100 more blocks are found (about 4 hours).
After that you can spend them. This is the same rule as Litecoin.


MINE AUTOMATICALLY EVERY TIME THE APP OPENS (optional)
------------------------------------------------------
1. Settings > Options > "Open Configuration File".
2. Add these 3 lines (with your address), then save and restart FierCoin:

      gen=1
      genaddress=firc1qYOURADDRESSHERE
      genproclimit=2


STEP 5 - YOU'RE ON THE NETWORK (nothing to set up)
--------------------------------------------------
There is ONE FierCoin network, shared by everyone, just like Bitcoin.
Install, open, and you're on it.
- Every FierCoin app has an always-on FierCoin computer built in (68.183.155.121, port 29333).
  Your wallet connects to it by itself, then learns about other FierCoin computers on its own.
- To check: rest your mouse on the little signal-bars icon in the bottom-right corner
  (don't click it). It shows how many "active connections to FierCoin network" you have.
  1 or more means you're on the network.
- You can turn your PC off any time. The network keeps going, and your wallet catches up
  the next time you open it.

Help others connect: leave your PC wallet running.
- Each PC that stays on is one more computer that new wallets can connect to and download
  the blockchain from. The more computers run FierCoin, the stronger and safer it gets.
- The Windows wallet opens your router for this by itself (this is called "UPnP"; most home
  routers allow it). You don't need to "port forward" or change any router settings.


ON AN ANDROID PHONE
-------------------
1. On your phone, download "FierCoin-Android.apk" from the FierCoin website and open it.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from this source, then tap "Install".
   It isn't from the Play Store, so you may see a warning. Tap "More details", then "Install anyway".
3. Open "FierCoin". It starts by itself and joins the FierCoin network.
   The top-right corner shows how many computers ("peers") you're connected to.
4. Make your wallet safe (do this first!): on the "Balance" tab, do the 2 steps,
   "Set a wallet password" and "Back up your wallet". The backup is saved in your
   Downloads folder. Copy it somewhere safe too, like a USB stick or your computer.
5. Go to the "Mining" tab and tap the big "Start mining" button. It works best while charging.
6. Do this once, so mining doesn't stop when the screen turns off:
   More > Settings > "Let FierCoin run in the background".

To update the app later: back up first, then install the new version and tap "Update".
Never uninstall first: that deletes your wallet.


ON AN IPHONE
------------
Apple doesn't allow mining apps in the App Store, so FierCoin for iPhone is a web app.
1. On your iPhone, open Safari and go to:  https://68-183-155-121.sslip.io/wallet/
2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
3. Close Safari and open "FierCoin" from your Home Screen.
   (Make your wallet there. A wallet made in Safari won't show up in the Home Screen app.)
4. Tap "Create a new wallet" and choose a password.
5. WRITE DOWN your 12 secret words on paper, in order. These 12 words ARE your coins:
   if you lose your phone, they're the only way to get your FierCoin back.
   Don't screenshot them. Never give them to anyone, even someone who says they're from FierCoin.
6. Go to the "Mining" tab and tap "Start mining". Keep the app open on screen while it mines:
   iPhone pauses mining when you leave the app or lock the screen. Plug your phone in.

- Your keys stay on your phone. The app works through the FierCoin server, which only sees
  your address, never your 12 words or your password.
- New phone? Add the web app to its Home Screen the same way, then tap "I already have 12 words".


LETTING FRIENDS JOIN
--------------------
There's nothing to set up, on your side or theirs:
1. Send them to the FierCoin website (or send them this guide).
2. They install FierCoin on their PC or phone and open it.
That's all. They're on the same network as you, so you can send each other coins right away.
(No IP addresses, no "port forwarding" and no "addnode" lines are needed any more.)


PRIVATE MODE (HIDE YOUR HOME ADDRESS)
-------------------------------------
Normally your wallet tells the FierCoin network your internet address (your "IP"), and the
Windows wallet opens a door on your router for other computers. Your IP shows roughly where
you live and who your internet provider is. Private mode sends everything through Tor
instead, so from then on other computers don't learn your IP. Mining and payments work the
same. If you used FierCoin before without private mode, other computers
may already have seen your address.

On your Windows PC:
1. Install Tor Browser (free, from torproject.org). Open it, click "Connect", and tick
   "Always connect automatically". Keep it open whenever FierCoin is running: FierCoin uses
   the Tor connection inside it.
2. Turn off Wi-Fi (or unplug the internet cable) BEFORE you open FierCoin, so it can't
   connect the normal way before you change its settings. Then open FierCoin and go to
   Settings > Options > Network.
   - Untick "Map port using UPnP".
   - Untick "Allow incoming connections".
   - Tick "Connect through SOCKS5 proxy (default proxy)". Proxy IP: 127.0.0.1  Port: 9150
3. Click OK and close FierCoin. Turn the internet back on, then open FierCoin again.
   Syncing is a little slower through Tor; that's normal.

Or, instead of the Network settings in step 2 (still offline), add these 3 lines to the
configuration file (Settings > Options > "Open Configuration File"), then close FierCoin and
turn the internet back on. Use one way or the other:

      proxy=127.0.0.1:9150
      listen=0
      upnp=0

If FierCoin shows 0 connections, Tor Browser isn't open or isn't connected yet. Open it,
click "Connect" and wait a minute. If Tor is blocked where you live, click "Configure
Connection" in Tor Browser and choose a bridge: it gets around the block.

On an Android phone:
The Android app is not private yet: even through Tor, it can still tell other computers your
phone's address, and on home Wi-Fi your home address. If your safety depends on hiding where
you are, don't use the Android app; use the PC in private mode instead. Orbot (free, from
the Tor Project) helps only partly: turn on its VPN mode and choose FierCoin in its app list,
then in Android's Settings > Network & internet > VPN > Orbot, turn on "Always-on VPN" and
"Block connections without VPN". Use mobile data, not home Wi-Fi.

On an iPhone:
Open the wallet address in "Onion Browser" (free, App Store) instead of Safari. Then the
FierCoin server sees a Tor address, not yours. Make your wallet there, and write down your
12 words as usual. It can't be added to the Home Screen, so open Onion Browser each time.

What private mode does NOT hide:
- Your payments. Every payment is still public on the blockchain. Private mode hides WHERE
  you are, not WHAT you paid.
- That you use Tor. Your internet provider can see you're connected to Tor (not what for).
  A bridge (see above) makes that much harder to see.
- Your PC itself. Anyone with your PC or phone can open the wallet, so keep the password.

Running a FierCoin server? The installer sets up Tor for you. At the end it prints a line
"Private (Tor) address: ....onion:29333". Friends in private mode can add it to their
configuration file as "addnode=....onion:29333" to reach your server inside Tor.
This does not hide the server: its normal IP address is still public.


NO INTERNET? PAY OVER MESH RADIO
--------------------------------
When there's no internet (a blackout, no cell service, or a network that's been switched
off), FierCoin can still travel over Meshtastic radios: small radios (about the size of a
phone) that pass short text messages to each other, often several kilometres per hop. One
computer somewhere in the mesh that still has internet runs the "FierCoin mesh gateway"
and passes payments on. Your 12 words and password never leave your phone.

Get ready while you still have internet:
1. Get a Meshtastic radio (for example a Heltec V3, LILYGO T-Beam or RAK WisBlock; in the
   US choose 915 MHz) and the free Meshtastic app. Pair them, set the region, and join the
   private channel of the person who runs the gateway (they share a QR code).
2. Open the FierCoin web wallet once with internet. It saves a copy of itself on your phone,
   so it opens later with no internet. (Use the web wallet in Safari or Chrome; the Android
   app doesn't have this yet.) On an iPhone, also add it to the Home Screen (Share > Add to
   Home Screen) and open it from there: Safari deletes the saved copy of a website you
   haven't opened for 7 days. Onion Browser can't keep a copy at all. Every month or so,
   turn on airplane mode and check that the wallet still opens.

To pay with no internet (web wallet: More > "No internet? Pay over mesh radio"):
1. Copy the message the wallet shows ("FIRC U ...") and send it in the Meshtastic app.
   Paste the gateway's answer ("FIRC u ..." lines) into the wallet and tap "Read my coins".
2. Tap "Make a payment" and fill it in as usual. The wallet shows a few short messages
   ("FIRC T ..."). Send each one in the Meshtastic app, in order.
3. The gateway answers "FIRC ok" (sent) or "FIRC err" (not sent, and why). Paste it into the
   wallet to check. Ask for your coins again before your next payment.
   Both answers can be faked by anyone on the channel. For anything that matters, have the
   person you paid check that the coins arrived. After "not sent", ask for your coins again
   before paying again: if the wallet says the coins are gone, the payment went through.
   If the wallet says a coin's amount changed, stop: someone is sending fake coin lists.

Good to know:
- It's slow: each radio message takes a few seconds or more.
- Only your 6 biggest coins come over the radio. Lots of small mined coins? Send everything
  to yourself in one payment while you still have internet.
- Use a private channel. On the default "LongFast" channel anyone nearby can read the messages.
- Radio signals can be traced to roughly where they come from.
Running a gateway: see "mesh-gateway/README.md" in the FierCoin source.

SENDING AND RECEIVING
---------------------
- To get paid: give people your "firc1..." address. On the PC it's on the Receive tab.
  On a phone, tap your FierCoin card (or "Receive") and let them scan your QR code.
- To pay someone on the PC: Send tab, paste their address and the amount, then click Send.
  On a phone: tap "Send", scan their QR code (or paste their address), type the amount, tap "Send".
- Sending is final, so check the address before you send.
- Freshly mined coins can be sent once 100 more blocks are found (about 4 hours).

Charging an amount, like a card machine (phone apps):
1. Tap your FierCoin card (or "Receive") and type the price under "Charge an amount".
2. The QR code now asks for that amount. When they scan it, the amount fills in by itself.
3. Wait for the big green "Paid". It shows a few seconds after they pay.
   "On its way" means the network hasn't confirmed it yet (that takes about 2.5 minutes).
   For a big amount, wait until it says "Confirmed by the network".
If it says the payment was cancelled, the money didn't arrive. Don't hand anything over.

Pay-me cards (phone apps):
On the Receive screen, tap "Print a pay-me card". Add a name if you like, such as your shop's
name. Each card shows your address and its QR code, so people can pay you by scanning it.
- "Print 10 cards" prints a page of business cards. Cut along the dashed lines, or use
  business-card sheets with 10 cards per page.
  On iPhone it opens the share menu with a PDF: tap "Print" there, or "Save to Files".
- "Save as a picture" saves one card, to send to people or print later.
The card shows only your address, never your 12 words or password. Losing one is safe.

HONEST WARNINGS
---------------
- FIRC has no price until people choose to trade it. Nobody can promise it will be worth $1.
- It is not on exchanges, in MetaMask, or on Uniswap. It has its own wallets (the FierCoin apps).
- Litecoin mining machines ("ASICs") also use Scrypt. If someone points one at FierCoin, they
  could win most blocks. Hourly difficulty adjustment keeps the network working if they leave.
- Some antivirus programs flag ANY program that can mine. If yours does, it's because of the miner.
- Keep your wallet backup and password safe. Nobody, including me, can recover lost coins.
