WHAT FIERCOIN IS
----------------
- Its own blockchain, made from Litecoin's code (just like Litecoin).
- Mined with your computer's processor using "Scrypt", the same mining method as Litecoin.
- 84,000,000 coins total, ever. That's the same as Litecoin.
- Each block pays the miner 50 FIRC. The reward is cut in half every 840,000 blocks (about every 4 years).
- A new block is found about every 2.5 minutes. Difficulty adjusts every hour, so it stays steady when miners come and go.
- Your wallet addresses start with "firc1" (or "F" for older-style ones).


STEP 1 - INSTALL
----------------
1. Double-click "fiercoin-win64-setup.exe".
2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". This happens because the app is new and not "signed".
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Click Next until it finishes, then open "FierCoin Core" from the Start menu.
4. On first launch, keep the default data folder and click OK.
5. Allow it through the Windows Firewall if asked, so other people's computers can connect to yours.


STEP 2 - MAKE YOUR WALLET SAFE (do this first!)
------------------------------------------------
1. Settings > Encrypt Wallet. Choose a strong password and WRITE IT DOWN on paper.
2. File > Backup Wallet. Save the backup to a USB stick. Keep it somewhere safe.
   (If your PC dies and you have no backup, your coins are gone forever.)
3. Never type your password or share your wallet file on any website or in any chat.


STEP 3 - GET YOUR ADDRESS
-------------------------
1. Click the "Receive" tab.
2. Click "Create new receiving address".
3. Click "Copy Address". It looks like:  firc1q...


STEP 4 - START MINING
---------------------
1. Click Window > Console.
2. Type this (paste YOUR address at the end) and press Enter:

      setgenerate true 2 firc1qYOURADDRESSHERE

   The "2" means it uses 2 processor threads. Use a bigger number to mine faster
   (your PC will get warmer and slower). Try 2 to 4.

3. To check how it's going, type:

      getgenerate

   "hashespersec" is your speed. "blocksfound" counts your wins.

4. To stop mining, type:

      setgenerate false

Note: freshly mined coins are "Immature" until 100 more blocks are found (about 4 hours).
After that you can spend them. This is the same rule as Litecoin.


MINE AUTOMATICALLY EVERY TIME THE APP OPENS (optional)
------------------------------------------------------
1. Settings > Options > "Open Configuration File".
2. Add these 3 lines (with your address), then save and restart FierCoin:

      gen=1
      genaddress=firc1qYOURADDRESSHERE
      genproclimit=2


STEP 5 - KEEP THE NETWORK ALIVE
-------------------------------
At the start, YOUR computer is the whole FierCoin network.
- If your computer is off, no new blocks are made. Nothing is lost; it continues when you turn it back on.
- The more computers run FierCoin, the stronger and safer it gets.


LETTING FRIENDS JOIN
--------------------
FierCoin has no "phone book" of computers yet, so friends have to be told where to find yours.

On YOUR side (one time):
1. Find your public IP address: search "what is my IP" on Google.
2. In your internet router's settings, "port forward" TCP port 29333 to your PC.
   (Search "port forwarding" + your router brand for steps.)

On your FRIEND's side:
1. They install FierCoin the same way.
2. Settings > Options > "Open Configuration File", then add this line, using YOUR IP:

      addnode=YOUR.IP.ADDRESS.HERE:29333

3. Save and restart FierCoin. Their wallet will download the blockchain from you.


SENDING AND RECEIVING
---------------------
- To get paid: give people your "firc1..." address (Receive tab).
- To pay someone: Send tab, paste their address and the amount, then click Send.


HONEST WARNINGS
---------------
- FIRC has no price until people choose to trade it. Nobody can promise it will be worth $1.
- It is not on exchanges, in MetaMask, or on Uniswap. It has its own wallet (this app).
- Litecoin mining machines ("ASICs") also use Scrypt. If someone points one at FierCoin, they
  could win most blocks. Hourly difficulty adjustment keeps the network working if they leave.
- Some antivirus programs flag ANY program that can mine. If yours does, it's because of the miner.
- Keep your wallet backup and password safe. Nobody, including me, can recover lost coins.
