First: add FierCoin to your Home Screen

This makes FierCoin work like a normal app, and keeps your wallet safe on your iPhone.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open FierCoin from your Home Screen.

Important: a wallet made here in Safari won't show up in the Home Screen app. Make your wallet in the Home Screen app.

Welcome to FierCoin

A free wallet and miner for FierCoin (FIRC). Your coins are protected by 12 secret words that only you will know.

Step 1: choose a password

You'll need it to send coins from this phone. At least 6 characters.

Step 2: write down your 12 words

These words are your coins. If you lose your phone, they're the only way to get your FierCoin back.

Write them on paper, in order. Never type them into any website or send them to anyone, including anyone saying they're from FierCoin. Don't screenshot them.
