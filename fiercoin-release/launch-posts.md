## 1. Bitcointalk: Announcements (Altcoins)

**Title:** [ANN] FierCoin (FIRC) | Scrypt PoW | No premine, no ICO | Mine on phone or PC

**Body:**

FierCoin is a new proof-of-work blockchain built from Litecoin Core 0.21.4.

**Specs**
- Algorithm: Scrypt (same as Litecoin)
- Block time: 2.5 minutes, difficulty retarget every 24 blocks (1 hour)
- Reward: 50 FIRC per block, halving every 840,000 blocks
- Max supply: 84,000,000 FIRC
- Launched: 13 Nov 2026
- Genesis: "FierCoin 13/Nov/2026 Regaining our democracy in the USA"

**Fair start, said plainly**
- No premine, no ICO, no dev fee. Every coin was mined.
- I was nearly the only miner for the first week, so I hold most of the early coins
  (about 55,000 so far). Every balance is visible on the explorer.
- More than 99.9% of all FIRC is still unmined.

**Links**
- Website and downloads: https://nest4fier.github.io/gittest/fiercoin/
- Explorer: http://68.183.155.121/
- Source code: https://github.com/NEST4FIER/gittest/tree/claude/salon-work-website-95l1m7/fiercoin-release
- Seed node: 68.183.155.121:29333

**Wallets**
- Windows wallet with a built-in CPU miner (`setgenerate true 2 YOUR_ADDRESS`)
- Android app: wallet, one-tap miner, QR send/receive, explorer

FIRC has no price and is not on any exchange. It's an experiment for people who
like mining and want a small, fair, open coin. Feedback and pool or node operators welcome.
